How It Works

Generate your code above, then text, email, or show your QR code to friends. Cards are placed in every freshly detailed car!

They Save $25

When they scan the QR code and complete a booking, $25 is taken off their total at checkout.

Once their service is completed, you earn $25 toward your next detail. Refer 3 = $75 off!

🔒 How We Keep It Fair
  • Credits are earned after service, not just booking. Discounts are only issued once the referred customer's detail is finished — not when the form is submitted.
  • All referrals are manually verified. We cross-check names, addresses, and vehicle info. Fake bookings are rejected — you cannot game the system by submitting phony forms.
  • Self-referrals don't count. Referring yourself under a different name will be caught during verification. Only genuine new customers qualify.
  • Stacking codes are owner-issued only. After 3 verified, completed referrals, we personally send you a unique stacking code — nobody can generate one on their own.
